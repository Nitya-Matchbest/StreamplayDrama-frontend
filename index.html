Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about building your drama streaming platform with StreamPlay.

How long does it take to develop an OTT app? +
Developing a custom OTT app from the ground up takes time. But it's also possible to get an amazing platform much faster, depending on your requirements. Usually, it takes around 3-5 working weeks to create an OTT app with customizations in features and additional integrations, if any, needed.
Will my video content support across all streaming devices? +
Yes, of course. StreamPlay facilitates its clients with the capability of supporting numerous content seamlessly with multi-viewing compatibility across all screens (Web, iOS, Android, Smart TVs). When content businesses decide to create their own movie streaming website, they can broadcast their valuable content effortlessly, removing all barriers.
What are the customization options available? +
StreamPlay provides extensive customization options, allowing you to develop a fully scalable streaming solution. You can customize elements like design themes, platform integration options, backend & frontend features, monetization models, and metadata structures tailored for Drama platforms.
What are the revenue models available? +
There are many monetization models available to assist you in stabilizing your revenue. Generally, production companies rely on prime monetization models such as Subscription Video on Demand (SVOD), Advertising VOD (AVOD), Transactional VOD (TVOD), Premium VOD, and Catch-Up TV.
How can I protect my video content with DRM? +
Since online piracy is a massive issue, end-to-end digital encryption is a vital need. With StreamPlay, your content is completely protected with multi-DRM security against theft or unauthorized breaches. Protocols like AES encryption, secure paywalls, and screen-recording prevention provide ironclad protection against copyright infringement.
