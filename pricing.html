Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Zero Revenue Sharing" mean?
It means exactly what it sounds like. We charge a flat licensing fee for our platform software. You keep 100% of the money you make from subscriptions (SVOD), ads (AVOD), and pay-per-view (TVOD). No hidden cuts.
Can I test the platform before committing?
Yes! We offer a full-featured Self-Serve Demo Environment and a 14-Day Free Trial sandbox. You can upload content, organize multi-season series, and preview how it looks on a live streaming app.
How long does it take to launch?
Because our OTT aggregator platform is pre-built, we can deploy a fully white-labeled version of the super app—with your branding, custom integrations, and 12+ content sources—in just 4 to 6 weeks.
Do I own the source code?
Absolutely. Our True White-Label model means you retain full intellectual property ownership of the resulting application code, branding, and customizations.
